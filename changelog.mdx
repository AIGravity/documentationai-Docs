## Release history

<Update label="2026-07-13" description="Bounty funding">

### Bounty pay-to-post funding via Razorpay

Bounty creators can now fund their bounties through Razorpay before they go live. When Razorpay is configured, bounties with a prize amount are created in `pending_payment` status and only become visible after payment is verified.

Changes:

- Bounty creators must pay the prize amount through Razorpay Checkout before a bounty goes live
- New API endpoints for creating and verifying bounty payment orders
- Bounty creation response now includes `paymentRequired`, `amount`, and `currency` fields
- Bounties in `pending_payment` status are hidden from the public bounty list
- Idempotency guard prevents duplicate bounty creation and double charges per session
- Posted bounty card is automatically rebuilt from the database after payment, surviving page reloads
- Razorpay Checkout SDK preloads on the bounty preview step for instant modal opening
- Razorpay webhook handler now routes bounty payment events alongside existing credits events
- When Razorpay is not configured, bounties are created as `open` immediately (free or development mode)


</Update>
