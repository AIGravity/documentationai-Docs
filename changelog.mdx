## Release history

<Update date="2026-07-11">

### New Features

- **30-day revenue chart** — The admin billing dashboard now displays a continuous 30-day daily revenue trend chart, replacing the previous static comparison view.
- **Revenue split by billing surface** — Unit Economics now separates revenue into Chat and Marketplace categories so you can see which surface drives earnings.
- **Week-over-week gross margin trend** — A trend badge appears next to gross margin, showing whether margin improved or declined versus last week.
- **Per-1,000-runs economics** — Revenue, LLM cost, and margin are now normalized per 1,000 runs for easier comparison at scale.
- **Refund statistics** — A new "Refunded" card in Cash and Liabilities shows total refunds, refund count, and refund rate as a percentage of revenue.

### Updates

- **Monetary values now display in USD** — All figures on the billing dashboard show dollar amounts instead of raw credit units.
- **Data cap warning banner** — A prominent banner appears when dashboard totals are approximate due to a scan limit, making clear the numbers are a minimum, not exact.
- **Color-coded KPI indicators** — Period-over-period change indicators on KPI cards now use green and red arrows for quick visual scanning.

### Bug Fixes

- **Charge settlement always recomputes from current pricing** — Charges are now calculated from live pricing data at settlement time rather than reusing pre-flight values, preventing potential over- and under-charging.



</Update>
