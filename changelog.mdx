## Release history

<Update label="2026-07-19" description="Leaderboard redesign" tags={["feature", "improvement", "bugfix"]}>

### New features

- The leaderboard locked screen now shows a full incentive showcase — perks, countdown timer, and opening date — before the competition goes live.
- A footer with branding and social links now appears consistently across all leaderboard phases.

### Updates

- Podium redesigned to highlight the top 3 positions; 4th and 5th place are noted below the stage.
- Leaderboard table now starts at rank 4, surfacing more builders than before.
- Perk cards now use uniform gold styling with a shine effect on hover.
- "Start Building" button now routes logged-in users directly to their dashboard instead of the onboarding page.
- Countdown clock adapts to each leaderboard phase: large before opening, compact during the live competition, hidden on final results.
- Removed the "It's real" navigation button from the leaderboard page.

### Bug fixes

- Fixed navigation bar positioning on the leaderboard page.
- Resolved scroll stuttering caused by the leaderboard wordmark glow animation.


</Update>
