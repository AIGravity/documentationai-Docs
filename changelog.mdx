## Release history

### July 19, 2026

**New features**
- **Leaderboard navigation in Builder Dashboard** — A dedicated Leaderboard link is now available in the Builder Dashboard sidebar, giving builders quick access to the leaderboard from anywhere in their workspace.

**Updates**
- **Redesigned Leaderboard perks cards** — The perks cards on the Leaderboard page now lead with concrete figures: revenue share percentage, per-agent bounties, and internship opportunities. The new layout makes rewards easier to scan at a glance.

**Fixes**
- **Locked leaderboard spacing** — Improved spacing between the perks section and the title on the locked leaderboard page for better readability.
