## Overview

This changelog tracks notable product changes. Entries are organized newest first, grouped by new features, updates, and bug fixes for quick scanning.

## Release history

### July 2026

**New Features**

- **Bounty search bar** — Search available bounties by name directly on the Bounties page. Results filter in real time and work alongside the existing difficulty filter.
- **Quality score visibility** — When an agent submission is auto-rejected for not meeting the quality threshold, your numeric score (out of 100) now appears alongside the rejection message — both on the review step and in your submission dashboard.

**Bug Fixes**

- Removed the inaccurate submissions count from individual bounty detail pages.
